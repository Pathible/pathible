## Usage Notes

These soundbites are designed for executors who:
- Have just lost someone and are suddenly responsible for settling the estate
- Feel overwhelmed by legal, financial, and logistical obligations they never prepared for
- Need clarity and structure, not another to-do list
- May be professionals (attorneys, financial advisors) guiding families through the process

The emotional hook is **empathy through shared burden** - "We've been the executor too" establishes Pathible as a guide who has walked the same path, not a tool that adds more complexity.
