# PEACE Soundbites

> Generated: January 17, 2026
> Product: Pathible
> Target Audience: Millennials who've experienced (or dread) the chaos of sorting through a loved one's affairs
> The Hole We Own: The grief-compounded chaos of sorting through a loved one's mess because no one prepared

## Combined Narrative

Have you ever had to sort through a loved one's mess while grieving? We have. Use Pathible. Get everything in one place for your family. So they never have to.

       ↓

       ↓

---

## Usage Notes

These soundbites are designed for millennials who:
- Have personally experienced the chaos of a loved one's disorganized estate
- Are watching their parents age and dread the same chaos
- Don't want to repeat the cycle with their own families

The emotional hook is **empathy through shared experience** - "We have" establishes Pathible as a guide who understands, not a corporation selling software.
