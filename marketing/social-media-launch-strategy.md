## Visual Guidelines

### Brand Colors (for Graphics)

- **Forest Green:** #4B7F52 (Primary)
- **Gold:** #D4AF37 (Accent)
- **Sage:** #7BA083 (Secondary)
- **Warm Sand:** #F6F4F1 (Background)
- **Charcoal:** #2C2C2C (Text)

### Typography

- **Headlines:** Crimson Text (serif, elegant, traditional)
- **Body:** Inter or clean sans-serif
- **Avoid:** Overly modern, techy fonts

### Image Style

- Warm, natural lighting
- Families (multigenerational when possible)
- Books, journals, handwritten elements
- Nature imagery (trees, paths, light through windows)
- Avoid: Stock photo feeling, overly polished

2. Carousel slide template (consistent branding)
3. Announcement template (launch, features)

## Risk Mitigation

### LinkedIn Employment Considerations

Jim is a Director of Engineering at another company. LinkedIn content MUST:

- Frame Pathible as a personal passion project
- Never use promotional/sales language
- Focus on problem-solving and learning
- Avoid posting during work hours (use scheduled posts for lunch/evening)
- Never mention other employer or contrast with "day job"

### Negative Response Preparation

If someone criticizes the product or faith angle:

- Respond graciously and professionally
- Don't engage in debates
- Acknowledge their perspective
- Take conversations to DMs if needed

### Low Engagement Contingency

If posts aren't performing:

- Boost best-performing post with small ad spend ($25-50)
- Engage in relevant community hashtags/groups
- Reach out to faith-based micro-influencers
- Cross-promote between platforms
