# Personal Facebook Content (Jim's Account)

## Getting Back on Facebook After 10 Years

Since you haven't been active for a decade, your approach should be:

1. **Re-introduce yourself** before promoting anything
2. **Reconnect with your network** authentically
3. **Share your journey** as a person, not a pitch
4. **Let Pathible emerge naturally** from your story

The key difference from the brand page: This is YOU talking to friends, family, former colleagues, and acquaintances. Not a company talking to customers.

### Post 2: The Story Behind Pathible

**Date:** Launch day (January 1)
**Type:** Long-form personal story

#### Caption:

A few years ago, my wife's mom passed away.

In the middle of her grief, my wife had to become a detective. Where's the will? What accounts did Mom have? Is there life insurance? Who's the attorney? What were her final wishes?

Weeks of searching through boxes, guessing passwords, making calls. Chaos on top of grief.

I kept thinking: there has to be a better way. Families shouldn't inherit confusion along with their loss.

So I built one.

It's called Pathible. It's a secure place for families to organize their important documents, capture their stories, and document their wishes. That way, "someday," their loved ones get clarity instead of a crisis.

I've been working on this for a year, and today it's finally live.

I know this isn't a "fun" topic. Nobody wants to think about this stuff. (Trust me, "Hey honey, want to talk about what happens when we die?" is not a great date night opener. I've tested this theory.)

But that's exactly why it matters.

If this resonates with you, or if you know someone who's been through what we went through, I'd love to hear from you.

pathible.com

"A good man leaves an inheritance to his children's children." (Proverbs 13:22)

## Ongoing Content Ideas (After Launch Week)

### Monthly Themes:

**Month 1:** Founder journey, "what I'm learning building this"
**Month 2:** Faith + stewardship content
**Month 3:** Real stories (with permission) from families
**Month 4:** Behind-the-scenes product development

### Post Types That Work on Personal Facebook:

1. **Milestone updates** ("Just hit X users" or "Shipped a new feature")
2. **Questions** ("What would you want your family to know?")
3. **Scripture reflections** (Proverbs 13:22, Deuteronomy 6, Psalm 78)
4. **Real-life moments** (Times when you realize why this matters)
5. **Gratitude posts** (Thank early users, supporters, family)
6. **Dad jokes with a point** (Humor that connects to legacy/family)

## Tips for Personal Facebook Success

1. **Reply to every comment** (This is your personal network, not faceless followers)
2. **Be real, not polished** (Your friends know you; don't sound like a brand)
3. **Share the struggle** (Founder stories resonate more than success stories)
4. **Ask questions** (Facebook's algorithm rewards engagement)
5. **Don't post too often** (2-3 times per week max on personal account)
6. **Use photos when natural** (You working late, your family, your "why")
7. **Thank people publicly** (When friends share or support, acknowledge them)
8. **Use your humor** (Dad jokes are your superpower)

---

## What NOT to Do on Personal Facebook

- Don't post like a brand page (polished graphics, marketing-speak)
- Don't use hashtags (not really a Facebook thing on personal accounts)
- Don't post the same content as the Pathible brand page
- Don't spam your friends with daily sales pitches
- Don't be pushy about the Founders Rate
- Don't forget you're talking to real people who know you
- Don't be afraid to be the wise dad/Dumbledore figure people already see you as

---
