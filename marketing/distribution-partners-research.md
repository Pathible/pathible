## KEY MESSAGING THEMES

For all outreach, emphasize:

1. **Empathy-First Positioning** - Pathible was built for people who have experienced the chaos of sorting through a loved one's affairs
2. **Faith-Based Foundation** - Biblical stewardship principles guide the platform
3. **Complementary Tool** - Pathible enhances (not replaces) existing estate planning services
4. **Gen X Target Market** - Adults 45-60 caring for aging parents while supporting children
5. **Peace of Mind** - Help families prepare without the overwhelm
