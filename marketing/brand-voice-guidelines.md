# Pathible Brand Voice & Content Guidelines

## Overview

This document defines how Pathible communicates across all channels. Consistency in voice builds trust with our faith-based audience.

---

## Brand Voice Pillars

### 1. Warm, Not Corporate
- Write like you're talking to a friend over coffee
- Avoid jargon, buzzwords, and marketing speak
- Use contractions (we're, you'll, don't)
- Be human first, brand second

**Instead of:** "Leverage our comprehensive document management solution"
**Say:** "Keep your important documents in one place where your family can find them"

### 2. Faith-Rooted, Not Preachy
- Scripture should feel natural, not forced
- Faith is woven in, not tacked on
- We serve families of faith without excluding others
- Let values show through actions, not lectures

**Instead of:** "As Christians, we must prepare for eternity by organizing our earthly possessions"
**Say:** "A good man leaves an inheritance to his children's children. That includes wisdom, not just wealth."

### 3. Trustworthy, Not Salesy
- Lead with value, not urgency
- Be honest about what we offer (and don't offer)
- Build relationship before asking for action
- Transparency builds trust

**Instead of:** "DON'T MISS OUT! Limited time offer!!!"
**Say:** "Our Founders Rate is available through January 7th. We'd love to have you join us."

### 4. Hopeful, Not Morbid
- Focus on the gift you're giving, not the event triggering it
- Legacy planning is an act of love, not a dark topic
- Frame preparation as empowerment
- Celebrate the life being documented

**Instead of:** "When you die, your family will need to find your will"
**Say:** "Someday, your family will need to find things. Make sure they can."

## Writing Do's and Don'ts

### DO:
- Start with the reader's problem
- Use "you" and "your" more than "we" and "our"
- Include specific details that build credibility
- Ask questions that invite engagement
- End with a clear (but not pushy) next step
- Use active voice
- Break up long paragraphs
- Include scripture where natural

### DON'T:
- Use ALL CAPS for emphasis
- Overuse exclamation points!!!
- Use fear-based messaging
- Make promises we can't keep
- Assume everyone shares our faith (inclusive, not exclusive)
- Use death/morbidity as the hook
- Sound like a used car salesman
- Forget the human on the other side

## Scripture Usage Guidelines

### Anchor Scripture
**Proverbs 13:22** - "A good man leaves an inheritance to his children's children."
This is our theological foundation. Use it prominently but not exclusively.

### Supporting Scriptures (use occasionally)
- **Psalm 78:4** - "We will tell the next generation the praiseworthy deeds of the Lord"
- **Deuteronomy 6:7** - "Impress them on your children. Talk about them when you sit at home..."
- **Proverbs 22:1** - "A good name is more desirable than great riches"
- **2 Timothy 1:5** - "I am reminded of your sincere faith, which first lived in your grandmother Lois..."

### Guidelines
- Let scripture speak for itself—don't over-explain
- Use sparingly (1 per post max)
- Ensure relevance to the content
- Include reference (book, chapter:verse)
- Modern translations preferred (NIV, ESV, NLT)

## Content Formatting Standards

### Headlines
- Title case for major headlines
- Sentence case for subheads
- Keep under 60 characters for SEO

### Social Media
- Instagram: Break into short paragraphs, use line breaks
- Facebook: Can be longer, still use paragraphs
- Twitter: One thought per tweet, threads for complex ideas
- LinkedIn: Professional paragraphs, scannable structure

### Punctuation
- One space after periods
- Use em-dashes (—) for emphasis
- Oxford comma: Yes
- Contractions: Yes (we're, you'll, don't)

## Review Checklist

Before publishing any content, verify:

- [ ] Does this sound like a person, not a corporation?
- [ ] Is the faith connection natural, not forced?
- [ ] Am I leading with value, not a pitch?
- [ ] Is the tone hopeful rather than morbid?
- [ ] Would I share this with my own family?
- [ ] Is the CTA clear but not pushy?
- [ ] Does this match the platform's tone?
- [ ] Have I proofread for typos?

---

*These guidelines should be referenced for all Pathible content creation. When in doubt, ask: "Would this help a family? Would they trust us after reading this?"*
