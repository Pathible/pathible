Thanks for trusting us with your family's legacy.

Don't leave them a mess. Leave them a blessing.

— Jim
Founder, Pathible

## Blog Post Templates

### Template 1: How-To Post

**Structure:**
1. **Hook:** Why this matters
2. **The Problem:** What people struggle with
3. **The Solution:** Step-by-step guide
4. **Connection to Pathible:** How we help (soft)
5. **CTA:** Next step

---

### Template 2: Why Post

**Structure:**
1. **Hook:** Counterintuitive insight or bold statement
2. **Story:** Personal example or case study
3. **The Why:** Deep dive on reasoning
4. **Practical Application:** What to do about it
5. **CTA:** How Pathible helps (soft)

---

### Template 3: List Post

**Structure:**
1. **Hook:** Promise of value (X Things Every Family Should Know)
2. **List Items:** Each with brief explanation
3. **Wrap-Up:** Tie it together
4. **CTA:** Relevant next step
