# Pathible Social Media Content Calendar

Duration: 4 weeks, January 20 to February 16, 2026  
Platforms: Facebook company, Facebook personal, LinkedIn personal, X personal  
Frequency: 2 to 3 posts per week per platform  
Framework: StoryBrand rails using PEACE as message anchors, not a script

| Week | Theme                      | Focus                                                    |

| 1    | Heritage Vault, The Search | Document chaos during grief, the scavenger hunt          |

---

## Messaging rails

These replace the “use verbatim every post” requirement. They keep you consistent without sounding scripted.

### Problem rail

Sorting through a loved one’s mess while grieving

### Empathy rail

We have  
I have

### Authority rail

Built from lived experience  
Designed for the moment you are not there to explain your system  
Built for families, not folders

### Plan rail

A simple 3 step plan appears lightly across the month.

1. Capture essentials (will, insurance, accounts, key contacts)
2. Choose who can access them
3. Add one note or story in your voice

### Success rail

Clarity instead of chaos  
They grieve you, not the mess  
No hunting, no guessing

### CTA rail

No promised downloads, no comment to receive, no walkthrough promises.  
CTAs point to your Learn section or to pathible.com.

Safe CTA options:

- Learn more at pathible.com
- Read this in Learn: [article slug]
- Start with one document today
- What would your family struggle to find
- What would you want them to know in your own words

---

## Voice guide

### Company voice (Pathible Facebook)

- We voice
- Community focused
- Inviting and educational
- Ends with a gentle CTA to Learn or site

### Personal voice (Facebook personal, LinkedIn, X)

- I voice
- Founder story, grounded and vulnerable
- Questions that invite replies
- Light mention of Pathible when it fits naturally
