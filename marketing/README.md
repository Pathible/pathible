## Quick Reference

### Core Soundbites (PEACE Framework)

**Problem:** "Have you ever had to sort through a loved one's mess while grieving?"
**Empathy:** "We have."
**Answer:** "Use Pathible."
**Change:** "Get everything in one place for your family."
**End Result:** "So they never have to."

### Tagline

**"Don't leave them a mess. Leave them a blessing."**

### Founders Rate

- **Price:** $19.99/month forever (normally $49.99)
- **Duration:** January 1-7, 2026 only
- **CTA:** pathible.com

### Core Scripture

>
> - Proverbs 13:22

**Path + Bible** - "God gives us a path, not just for how we live, but for how we steward what we've been given."

## Important Notes

### LinkedIn Considerations

Jim is a Director of Engineering at another company. All LinkedIn content MUST be framed as a personal passion project, not promotional business content. See [launch-content-linkedin.md](./launch-content-linkedin.md) for risk mitigation guidelines.

### Engagement Priority

During launch week, prioritize responding to:

1. Direct questions about Pathible
2. Comments from potential customers
3. Shares and positive mentions
4. Faith-based connections

### Negative Response Protocol

- Respond graciously and professionally
- Don't engage in debates
- Take heated conversations to DMs
- If harassment occurs, document and block
