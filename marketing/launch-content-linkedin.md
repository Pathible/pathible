One week in.

Pathible (pathible.com) has been live for a few days now. Families are signing up. Stories are being captured. That alone makes the early mornings worth it.

Not sure what comes next. But grateful for this week.

## Optional Post: Feature Highlight (Only if engagement is strong)
**Date:** Use only if Post 1 and 2 perform well
**Tone:** Educational, thoughtful

### Post:

Something I learned building Pathible:

Most "document vault" tools focus on storage.

But storage isn't the problem. Finding things is.

The real challenge families face isn't "where do I put this?" It's "where did they put this?"—asked during the hardest moment of their lives.

So I built differently:

- Organized categories for the documents families actually need (wills, insurance, accounts)
- A "Document Access Map" so family members know where to find everything
- Space for context—not just files, but the story behind them

It's a small shift in thinking. But it's the difference between a filing cabinet and actual peace of mind.

If you're interested: pathible.com

But more than that—if you're building something yourself, think about this:

What's the real problem you're solving? Storage? Or something deeper?

The answer changes everything.

## Posts to AVOID on LinkedIn

Do NOT post:
- "Founders Rate ending soon!" (too promotional)
- "Sign up now!" (sounds like an ad)
- Multiple posts in one week (too much visibility)
- Anything that sounds like you're running a business
- Content during work hours that looks like you're distracted from your job
- Direct comparison to competitors
- "We're hiring" or "Join us" language

## Engagement Strategy

1. **Don't over-engage.** Unlike Twitter, you don't need to respond to every comment immediately.

2. **Keep responses brief and professional.** Thank people, answer questions, don't pitch.

3. **Like and respond to comments from colleagues carefully.** Keep it light and grateful.

4. **Don't share to LinkedIn groups.** This can look spammy and promotional.

5. **Let the content speak.** If it resonates, people will share. Don't ask for shares.
