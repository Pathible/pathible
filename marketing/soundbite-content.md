## Content Principles (Based on Soundbites)

1. **Lead with the problem** - Always start with the pain. It's specific and relatable.

2. **Establish empathy immediately** - "We have" creates instant connection. We're not selling—we're sharing.

3. **Keep the answer simple** - "Use Pathible" or "Get everything in one place." No features. No complexity.

4. **Make the vision personal** - "So they never have to" ties back to their experience.

5. **Zero cognitive load** - If someone has to think about what you mean, rewrite it.
