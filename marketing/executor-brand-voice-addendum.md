# Executor Brand Voice Addendum

> Product: Pathible Executor
> Framework: GRACE (Grief, Recognition, Answer, Clarity, Exhale)
> Last Updated: March 5, 2026

This document extends Pathible's brand voice for the Executor product. It should be read alongside the core brand guidelines and GRACE soundbites.

---

## Voice Adjustments

| Dimension | Planning (PEACE) | Executor (GRACE) |
|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Tense** | Future ("Your family will...") | Present ("You need to...") |
| **Focus** | "Your family" - protecting others | "You" - the executor IS the person in pain |
| **Tone** | Reflective motivation | Empathetic urgency |
| **Pace** | Thoughtful, long-horizon | Immediate, step-by-step |
| **Posture** | Coach ("Let's plan ahead") | Companion ("We'll walk with you") |

---

## Words to Use

| Use This | Instead Of | Why |
|----------|------------|-----|
| "settle an estate" | "administer" | Human language, not legal jargon |
| "the person you lost" | "the decedent" | Honors the relationship, not the legal status |
| "steps" / "path" | "tasks" / "to-dos" | A journey, not a project |
| "guidance" | "management" | We guide, we don't manage |
| "close" an estate | "complete" | Closing carries emotional weight and finality |
| "your responsibility" | "your burden" | Affirms capability, not victimhood |

---

## Words to Avoid

| Word | Why |
|------|-----|
| "Decedent" | Cold legal jargon that strips away personhood |
| "Probate" (without context) | Always explain what it means when first used |
| "Simple" or "easy" | It is neither. Never minimize the experience. |
| "Just" | "Just file the paperwork" minimizes real difficulty |
| "Tasks" / "to-dos" | Feels corporate and impersonal for grief-adjacent work |
| "Optimize" / "streamline" | This is not a productivity problem |

## Faith Integration

Faith references in the Executor product should be **more subtle** than in Planning. The executor may not share the faith of the person who died. Faith should comfort, not preach.

### Appropriate Scripture References

- **"Carry each other's burdens, and in this way you will fulfill the law of Christ."** (Galatians 6:2) - Validates the executor's sacrifice
- **"Well done, good and faithful servant."** (Matthew 25:21) - Affirms faithful stewardship
- **"The Lord is close to the brokenhearted."** (Psalm 34:18) - Meets grief with presence

### Guidelines

- Use scripture as comfort, never as obligation
- Place faith elements where users opt in (not in onboarding or core UI)
- The executor may be serving someone else's faith - honor that distinction
- Avoid "God has a plan" framing when someone is grieving

## Emotional Register

| | Planning | Executor |
|---|----------|----------|
| **Core emotion** | Prevention | Empowerment |
| **Headline voice** | "You know how hard it was. Don't pass that on." | "You didn't ask for this. But you can handle it." |
| **Motivation** | Fear of repeating the past | Confidence to face the present |
| **Relationship to pain** | Remembers past pain, acts to prevent | Currently in pain, needs support now |
| **Call to action** | "Start planning today" | "Take your first step" |

---

## Cross-Product Voice Consistency

What both products share:

- **Empathy-first approach** - Lead with understanding, not features
- **"We've been there" credibility** - Shared experience is our authority
- **Simplicity over jargon** - If a lawyer would say it, we probably shouldn't
- **Respect for the weight** - Death, grief, and legacy are serious. Our voice reflects that.

What both products avoid:

- **Corporate speak** - No "solutions," "leverage," or "synergize"
- **Fear-mongering** - We don't manufacture urgency or guilt
- **Over-promising** - We guide, we don't guarantee outcomes
- **Toxic positivity** - "Everything happens for a reason" has no place here
