## PEACE Soundbites (Use Verbatim - Every Post)

These exact phrases must appear in virtually every post:

| Element | Company Voice | Personal Voice |
|---------|---------------|----------------|
| **Problem** | "Have you ever had to sort through a loved one's mess while grieving?" | Same |
| **Empathy** | "We have." | "I have." |
| **Answer** | "Use Pathible." | "That's why I built Pathible." |
| **Change** | "Get everything in one place for your family." | Same |
| **End Result** | "So they never have to." | Same |

**Rule:** Every post should include at least 2-3 of these soundbites. The full sequence should appear multiple times per week.

---

## Voice Guide

**Company (Pathible Facebook):**
- "We" voice
- Community-focused
- Inviting, educational
- Always ends with soundbite + CTA

**Personal (Jim's Facebook, LinkedIn, X):**
- "I" voice
- Founder journey, vulnerable
- Questions to draw people in
- Always anchored by soundbites
