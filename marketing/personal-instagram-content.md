# Personal Instagram Content (Jim's Account)

## Getting Back on Instagram After 10 Years

Instagram has changed dramatically since you were last on it. Here's what to know:

### The Platform Today:
- **Reels** (short videos) dominate, but you don't have to make them
- **Stories** (24-hour posts) are casual and low-pressure
- **Feed posts** still matter but engagement is lower than it used to be
- **Carousels** (multi-image posts) perform well
- **Hashtags** still help discovery but less than before

### Your Strategy:
1. Start simple. No pressure to make Reels immediately
2. Use Stories for casual, behind-the-scenes content
3. Post to your feed 2-3x per week max
4. Let your personality shine through (humor + wisdom)
5. Connect with others in the faith/entrepreneurship space

## Instagram Stories Strategy

Stories are perfect for your return. They're casual, temporary, and forgiving.

### Daily Story Ideas:

**Morning:**
- Coffee + "Working on Pathible today"
- Scripture that's on your mind
- What you're grateful for

**Throughout the day:**
- Behind-the-scenes of building (code, notes, whiteboard)
- Quick thoughts (text on plain background)
- Reposts of Pathible content with your take
- Random dad joke (because why not)

**Evening:**
- "What I learned today"
- Family moments (if comfortable sharing)
- Questions for your audience

### Story Features to Use:

- **Polls:** "Have you organized your important documents?" (Yes/I should)
- **Questions:** "What would you want your family to know about you?"
- **Link stickers:** Direct links to pathible.com
- **Countdown:** For Founders Rate deadline

## Instagram Dos and Don'ts

### DO:
- Be authentic. You're not a brand, you're a person
- Show your face occasionally
- Engage with comments and DMs
- Use Stories liberally (they disappear!)
- Cross-promote with @pathible.legacy occasionally
- Take your time. Quality over quantity
- Let your humor and wisdom come through

### DON'T:
- Over-polish everything
- Post the same content as the brand account
- Use stock photos
- Buy followers
- Stress about Reels (do them if you want, skip if not)
- Post more than once a day on your feed
- Hide your personality

## Getting Comfortable Again

Since you've been away for 10 years, start slow:

**Week 1:** Just post and reply to comments
**Week 2:** Start using Stories
**Week 3:** Engage with others' content (like, comment)
**Week 4:** Find your rhythm

No pressure to be an influencer. You're just a guy sharing something meaningful. With occasional dad jokes.
