## Email Formatting Notes

### Styling Guidelines:
- **From Name:** Jim Gibbs or Jim from Pathible
- **From Email:** jim@pathible.com (or hello@pathible.com)
- **Reply-To:** Same as from email (Jim reads replies)
- **Format:** Plain text preferred, or minimal HTML with brand colors
- **CTA Buttons:** Forest Green (#4B7F52) background, white text
- **Font:** System fonts or Inter for consistency

### Segmentation:
- If possible, segment between "opened previous emails" vs "hasn't engaged"
- More engaged subscribers → all 5 emails
- Less engaged → emails 1, 4, 5 only (launch, urgency, thank you)

### Tracking:
- Add UTM parameters to all links: `?utm_source=email&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=launch2026`
- Track: open rate, click rate, conversion (sign-ups)

## Subject Line Best Practices

**What works for this audience:**
- Personal, conversational tone
- Curiosity-driven (not clickbait)
- Clear value or urgency
- Scripture references (sparingly)
- Avoid: ALL CAPS, excessive punctuation, spam trigger words

**A/B Testing Priority:**
- Test subject lines on Email 1 (largest send)
- Use winner pattern for subsequent emails
