## What Pathible Does

Pathible is a production-ready platform that helps Christian families organize their legacy — documents, finances, wishes, values, and stories — in one secure place.

**Heritage Vault** — Secure cloud storage for wills, insurance policies, deeds, and family photos. Everything a family needs, organized and accessible to the people who matter.

**Legal Document Wizards** — Step-by-step guidance for creating wills, trusts, powers of attorney, and healthcare directives. State-specific requirements for all 50 states. Generates PDF drafts families can take to their attorney.

**Wisdom & Values** — A place to capture family stories, life lessons, core beliefs, and letters to future generations. The things that matter most and can't be replaced.

**Financial Overview** — Families document their accounts, properties, and insurance policies in one place. No more searching through filing cabinets during the hardest season of life.

**Family Management** — Invite family members with role-based access. Parents manage, adult children view, executors access what they need.
